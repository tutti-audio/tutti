refactor(session,sampler,codec)!: modulation is always on; synth implies midi; sampler forces no decoder

Modulation and automation are always built: `tutti-modulation` is a
dependency of `tutti-session` with no feature, `PhaseCx` has its `mods`
field, `Phase::Modulation` always runs, and every `cfg(feature =
"modulation")` is gone.

`synth` implies `midi`. `sampler` forces no decoder: the `audio_file`
entry, `AudioEntry`, `StreamInfo` and `Tier` exist when `tutti-codec` is
built with a `decode-*` feature (`cfg(codec)`, read from the codec's
`links` metadata), and `tutti-sampler`'s `probe` follows the same cfg.
`tutti-sampler`'s forwarding `decode-*` features are removed.

The clippy and nextest recipes and the CI feature lists drop the removed
names.

Removed features:
- `tutti-session/modulation`: always on.
- `tutti-sampler/decode-wav`, `decode-flac`, `decode-mp3`, `decode-ogg`:
  turn on `tutti-codec`'s of the same name.
- `tutti-session/sampler` no longer turns on `decode-wav`: turn on
  `tutti-codec/decode-wav`.
