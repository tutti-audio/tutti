# Ids:
#   m2-123/<section>#<n>          the nth sentence under the numbered section
#                                 (appendix letters lowercase: d.2.1) that carries
#                                 a conformance word. kind: shall is required,
#                                 should recommended, may permitted; the negated
#                                 forms keep the kind and carry the fact neg=1.

# rows as M2-104's, and no negated forms either. Its Table 3 lists must,
# will, can and might as words that are never conformance statements: a

# document has a row per item.
